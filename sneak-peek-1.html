📖 Sneak Peek #1

The Journeys That Choose You

A first look inside We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability by Kim Alfonso, MBA.

There are some journeys you plan for.

And then there are the journeys that choose you.

When our daughter Allie was diagnosed with a disability, we entered a world we knew very little about. Suddenly, there were doctors, specialists, school meetings, accommodations, questions—and decisions we never imagined we would have to make.

We didn’t have a roadmap.

What we did have was a little girl who would eventually teach us that her possibilities were far greater than our fears.

This book is about that journey—the uncertainty, the advocacy, the mistakes, the victories, and the lessons we learned along the way.

And perhaps the biggest lesson of all:

We weren’t just raising our daughter. She was raising us.

💜 This is just a glimpse. More of our journey is coming soon.

— Kim Alfonso, MBA
