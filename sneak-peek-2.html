❤️ Sneak Peek #2: The Journey We Never Expected

The Day I Realized Our Journey Would Be Different

From We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability by Kim Alfonso, MBA.

When you become a parent, you dream about your child’s future.

You imagine their first steps, their first day of school, their friendships, their accomplishments, and all the wonderful possibilities ahead.

What you don’t imagine is sitting in a doctor’s office hearing that your child has a disability.

When we learned that our daughter, Allie, was visually impaired, suddenly there were so many questions.

Would she be able to attend a regular school? Would she make friends? Would she be independent? What would her future look like?

I didn’t have the answers. And I certainly didn’t have a roadmap.

But here’s something I learned along the way:

A diagnosis may change the journey, but it doesn’t determine the destination.

Over the years, I discovered that raising a child with a disability isn’t just about helping your child overcome challenges. It’s also about changing your own expectations, discovering possibilities you never imagined, and learning when to hold on and when to let go.

And perhaps my greatest discovery was this:

I thought I was raising and teaching Allie. But in so many ways, she was raising and teaching me.

❤️ That’s part of the story I share in my upcoming book, We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability.

— Kim Alfonso, MBA
