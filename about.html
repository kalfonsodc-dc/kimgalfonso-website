About Kim

Mother, Advocate, Business Leader

Kim Greenfield Alfonso, MBA, has spent more than 30 years working across the public, private, and nonprofit sectors in the Washington, DC region—and more than two decades as an advocate for people with disabilities.

Where the journey began

Kim’s foray into the disability field began when her daughter was born. She was born with Peters Anomaly, which resulted in her having limited vision, being a braille reader, and a cane traveler. Like many parents who have a child with a disability, Kim immersed herself in learning as much as possible to become the best advocate for her child.

For more than 20 years since, Kim has worked in the blindness arena on boards and parent organizations, including the Montgomery County Commission for People with Disabilities, the Maryland School for the Blind, and the D.C. Advisory Board for Special Education. She spent 12 years as Chief Operating Officer at the Columbia Lighthouse for the Blind.

That road—the diagnosis, the school meetings, the advocacy, and watching her daughter grow into an independent young woman—is the heart of her forthcoming book, We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability.

“We weren’t just raising our daughter. She was raising us.”
Why accessibility is personal

The evening that changed everything

“My heart was pounding one evening, and I was unsuccessful in fighting back the tears as I watched my daughter trying so hard independently to work on a research project. She struggled to navigate a website that was not accessible, and a research project that should have taken anyone else just one hour took her three hours. She had to finally ask for my assistance.”

“This topic of accessibility and inclusion is personal to me as I have watched my daughter through the years struggle with online accessibility issues—during school and college, applying for scholarships and internships, accessing school materials, and most recently, trying to access a list of COVID testing sites online that was inaccessible to her.”

That experience led Kim to co-found Results One LLC (opens in new window), with a mission to create an accessible and inclusive world where everyone is All In.

Professional background

Leading with experience

Kim is the co-founder and Chief Executive Officer of Results One LLC, where she helps organizations move from awareness to action through digital accessibility testing, document remediation, training, policy and program development, and ongoing consulting. After a successful career in the nonprofit and corporate arena, Kim knows that a diverse workforce is critical to the success of all organizations, and that diversity and inclusion are the cornerstones of a productive environment.

She also writes The Digital Accessibility Lady blog, which breaks down accessibility laws, inclusive design, and assistive technology in a clear, approachable way.
