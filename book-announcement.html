Kim Alfonso Shares a Powerful and Personal Story of Parenting, Advocacy, and Raising a Child with a Disability in New Book

We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability offers encouragement, insight, and practical guidance for families navigating the unexpected journey of disability.

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Author, business leader, and disability advocate Kim Greenfield Alfonso, MBA, announces the publication of her deeply personal book, We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability. Drawing from more than two decades of personal experience raising a daughter who is blind, Alfonso offers an honest and heartfelt perspective on the challenges, lessons, and unexpected rewards of parenting a child with a disability.

A child’s disability diagnosis can change the course of a family’s life. Suddenly, parents find themselves navigating unfamiliar medical terminology, educational systems, therapies, accommodations, and advocacy responsibilities while processing their own emotions and adjusting to a future they may never have imagined.

For many families, the journey can feel isolating. The questions are endless, the road is unfamiliar, and finding the right resources and support can be overwhelming.

Alfonso knows this journey firsthand.

As the mother of a daughter who is blind, she has experienced the realities of raising a child with a disability, from early medical uncertainty and navigating educational systems to advocating for equal opportunities, encouraging independence, and learning when to protect and when to let go.

In We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand, she shares the lessons she has learned along the way, offering readers a perspective that goes beyond the challenges of disability. The book explores the importance of self-advocacy, building confidence, challenging assumptions, creating opportunities, and recognizing the possibilities that exist when children with disabilities are given the support and independence to thrive.

“One of the most important things I’ve learned is that our children are capable of far more than we sometimes imagine. As parents, our responsibility is not simply to protect them from the world, but to prepare them to navigate it, advocate for themselves, and discover their own possibilities.”

More than a personal account, the book serves as a source of encouragement and understanding for parents who are just beginning their journey, caregivers learning to navigate complex systems, and families working to balance protection with independence.

It also offers valuable perspective for educators, relatives, professionals, and anyone who wants to better understand the experiences of families raising children with disabilities.

Through her story, Alfonso hopes to remind families that they are not alone, that their experiences matter, and that a diagnosis does not define a child’s potential or determine the limits of their future.

A Message That Extends Beyond the Book

Alfonso’s commitment to disability advocacy extends beyond her role as a mother and author. As a digital accessibility professional and advocate for inclusion, she has dedicated her work to helping organizations remove barriers and create more accessible opportunities for people with disabilities.

Her personal experiences have shaped her belief that inclusion must go beyond good intentions. It requires understanding, advocacy, meaningful opportunities, and a willingness to challenge the systems and assumptions that create barriers.

With this book, Alfonso hopes to encourage more open conversations about disability, empower parents to become confident advocates, and help communities better understand the realities of raising a child with a disability.

We’re Off on a Journey Few Understand: Raising a Child with a Disability is a reminder that while families may not choose the journey, they can choose how they travel it.

About Kim Alfonso

Kim Greenfield Alfonso, MBA, is a business leader, disability advocate, digital accessibility professional, and author. Her professional work focuses on helping organizations create inclusive and accessible digital experiences while removing barriers that prevent people with disabilities from fully participating in society.

As the mother of a daughter who is blind, Kim brings both professional expertise and deeply personal experience to her advocacy. Her journey has shaped her passion for accessibility, independence, equal opportunity, and empowering families to navigate the challenges of raising children with disabilities.

Through her work and writing, Kim hopes to inspire individuals, families, and organizations to see beyond limitations and recognize the possibilities that inclusion, understanding, and opportunity can create.
